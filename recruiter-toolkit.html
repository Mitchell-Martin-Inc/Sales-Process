Conversations with candidates

          
Ask better questions, then listen.

          
Use this flow to prepare with the salesperson, run stronger candidate discovery calls, and explain benefits or salary model details clearly.

        

        

          

            
Understand the req first

            
    
              
  • Confirm client specifics, day-to-day work, required skills, nice-to-have skills, certifications, location, rate, markup, pricing, and rules of engagement.
    • 
              
  • Ask the AE what the ideal candidate looks like and what would put someone over the top.
    • 
              
  • Ask whether placements have been made here before and why the role is open.
    • 
              
  • Confirm the interview process before recruiting.
    • 
              
  • If something is unclear, ask and repeat it back at the end.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Open the candidate call

            
    
              
  • Introduce who you are, why you are calling, and the value you can add.
    • 
              
  • Ask what brings them to the job market.
    • 
              
  • If currently employed, ask what is causing them to look for a new opportunity.
    • 
              
  • Clarify whether they are actively or passively looking.
    • 
              
  • Build rapport and keep yourself to 40% or less of the talking.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Discovery questions

            
    
              
  • Identify hot buttons: compensation, remote/onsite, commute, work-life balance, manager, team, job function, and responsibilities.
    • 
              
  • Validate name, address, phone, email, preferred contact method, best hours, current title, years of experience, certifications, and work authorization.
    • 
              
  • Use if/then questions to separate needs from wants.
    • 
              
  • Ask where else they have been submitted, interviewed, and where they are in the decision process.
    • 
              
  • Ask when they can interview, start, whether they need notice, and whether they have planned time off.
    • 
            

          

          

            
KYC and close-forward notes

            
    
              
  • Ask why they are looking for a new opportunity.
    • 
              
  • Ask the three things that motivate them when thinking about a new role.
    • 
              
  • Ask how they would rank the opportunities presented to them.
    • 
              
  • Summarize their why back to them.
    • 
              
  • Recap the conversation, confirm next steps, set a follow-up, and take strong notes in JobDiva.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Benefits conversation

            
    
              
  • Hourly and salaried consultant benefit guides both note eligibility for full-time employees working 30 or more hours per week.
    • 
              
  • Coverage is effective the first of the month following 30 days for benefit-eligible employees electing coverage.
    • 
              
  • Qualified dependents include spouse, children up to age 26, certain incapacitated children, and domestic partners.
    • 
              
  • Qualifying events must be reported to HR within 30 days.
    • 
              
  • Use the benefit guides for the governing details and plan specifics.
    • 
            

            

              Hourly guide
              Salary guide
            

          

          

            
Salary model reminders

            
    
              
  • Use the salary model for exceptions and manager approval conversations.
    • 
              
  • Inputs include hourly bill rate, discount, annual salary, per diem, payroll tax load, medical cost, immigration costs, referral fees, and other expenses.
    • 
              
  • The model analyzes gross profit and flags low-profit deals requiring manager approval.
    • 
              
  • Salary terms convert total compensation and per diem into hourly equivalents.
    • 
              
  • Use the model to support clear conversations about bill, pay, costs, and gross profit.
    •

Candidate ownership

          
Track, attribute, and protect ownership.

          
All candidates and jobs must be tracked in JobDiva so ownership rules can be administered correctly. True ownership comes from meaningful candidate qualification, updated records, and documented activity.

        

        

          Ownership guide
        

        

          

            
Core ownership rules

            
    
              
  • All candidates must be tracked in JobDiva, even when interviews are scheduled outside the system.
    • 
              
  • All jobs must be entered in JobDiva to support proper ownership rules.
    • 
              
  • Phone qualification at length for a current or future MMI client position is a true attribute signal.
    • 
              
  • Active email, phone number, and an accurate updated resume support complete attributes.
    • 
              
  • If a candidate is not tracked, ownership may split with the recruiter who tracks them first in JobDiva.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Attributes collected

            
    
              
  • Current situation and real reason for looking.
    • 
              
  • Most important detail for an ideal new role: industry, size, culture, or similar.
    • 
              
  • Top skills and strengths, minimum 3 to 5.
    • 
              
  • Role type preference: direct hire, contract-to-hire, or contract.
    • 
              
  • Employment status, visa status, compensation expectations, location, travel, relocation, interview activity, availability to start, and selling notes.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Existing candidates

            
    
              
  • For candidates with prior ownership, new ownership requires submission, new attributes, and updated contact information when provided.
    • 
              
  • Outside the 45-day contact window, refresh comp, interview activity, visa, reason for leaving, location, and new skills.
    • 
              
  • To take full ownership, the candidate must submit to a role.
    • 
              
  • Cross-reference JobDiva, LinkedIn, Dice, Monster, and other tools before reaching out.
    • 
              
  • A retention connect counts only when the candidate replies by email, call, LinkedIn, or text.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Placement ownership

            
    
              
  • Full-time placements have 12 months of ownership from placement with quarterly check-ins to maintain lifetime ownership.
    • 
              
  • CNS ownership continues during contract; after 45 days from end date, retention connects must happen every 45 days.
    • 
              
  • No active hunting or proactive outreach to a current/past owned candidate while protected.
    • 
              
  • Referral ownership depends on direct contact info, a recruiter connection, and resume collection.
    • 
            

          

        

      

      

        

          
Immigration, 3rd parties and ICs

          
Required documents and identity controls.

          
Use this section when working with H1B transfers, third-party corporations, IC corporations, or subcontractor identity validation.

        

        

          

            
3rd party corporations

            
    
              
  • MMI works with S-Corp, C-Corp, and qualifying LLC corporations with corporate election proof.
    • 
              
  • Corporations must file corporate taxes and provide proof, such as Federal 941 or a recent W2 issued by the corporation.
    • 
              
  • Corporations filing as 1099 are not accepted.
    • 
              
  • LLCs must provide Form 8832 or Form 2553 when applicable.
    • 
              
  • New corporations must provide the IRS EIN letter and the appropriate completed corporate election form.
    • 
            

            
Download docs

          

          

            
3rd party onboarding list

            
    
              
  • Signed agreement, term schedule, rider page, NDA, and client documents.
    • 
              
  • Two references, completed W-9, direct deposit form, and voided business check or bank letter.
    • 
              
  • Proof of corporate ownership and recent payroll tax filing or corporation-issued W2.
    • 
              
  • Completed I-9 and supporting IDs for the employee.
    • 
              
  • E-Verify approval document and certificate of insurance naming Mitchell Martin Inc. as additional insured.
    • 
            

            
Download docs

          

          

            
IC corporation requirements

            
    
              
  • S-Corp and C-Corp entities must show corporate business tax filing proof.
    • 
              
  • LLCs must show corporate election through Form 8832 or S-Corp election through Form 2553.
    • 
              
  • MMI will not engage ICs working in California or Wyoming.
    • 
              
  • Required documents include subcontractor agreement, NDA, client documents, two references, W-9, direct deposit, proof of ownership, tax filing proof, I-9 attestation, and insurance.
    • 
              
  • Insurance must name Mitchell Martin Inc. and the client as additional insureds.
    • 
            

            
Download requirements

          

          

            
H1B questionnaire

            
    
              
  • Collect candidate legal name, phone, client, position, recruiter, and current legal employment status.
    • 
              
  • Capture first U.S. entry, visa terms, initial H-1B status, cap filing, work start date, expiration, travel time, and max-out date.
    • 
              
  • Ask about prior H-1B denials and current work status.
    • 
              
  • If applicable, collect PERM, labor approval, priority date, I-140, EB category, and approval timing.
    • 
              
  • Misrepresentation can jeopardize the application and opportunity.
    • 
            

            
Download questionnaire

          

          

            
Identity attestation

            
    
              
  • Collect candidate legal name, subcontractor name, prospective job title, and prospective end-client name.
    • 
              
  • Authorized subcontractor representative acknowledges the candidate resume, education, and work history are true and accurate.
    • 
              
  • Candidate must be present for all interviews and, if engaged, report to the work site.
    • 
              
  • Subcontractor confirms they are the W2 employer of the candidate.
    • 
              
  • Misrepresentation may result in damages, client/MSP reporting, and no future work with the candidate or subcontractor.
    • 
            

            
Download attestation

          

        

      

      

        

          
Resume formatting

          
Client-ready resume SOP.

          
Before submission, remove personal information, follow the requested MMI branding direction, and make the resume clean enough for sales to send directly to the client.

        

        

          Ropes Companion can format your resumes very easily
          Use this SOP to guide the final review.
        

        

          Download NY Letterhead
          Resume SOP
        

        

          

            
Privacy & delivery

            
    
              
  • Remove all personal contact information before submission.
    • 
              
  • No home address, personal phone number, or personal email address.
    • 
              
  • Submit resumes as a Word document, .docx.
    • 
              
  • Coordinate with sales on any required header or footer before initial submission.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Letterhead & branding

            
    
              
  • Place the resume on MMI letterhead if sales requests it during the initial conversation.
    • 
              
  • Apply a header and/or footer when directed by the sales team for the specific submittal.
    • 
              
  • The goal is that sales can send the resume directly to the client with no additional editing.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Formatting standards

            
    
              
  • Use Arial, 10pt throughout.
    • 
              
  • Bold candidate name, section titles, company name, position, and dates.
    • 
              
  • Keep font, spacing, indentation, and alignment consistent throughout.
    • 
              
  • Eliminate excess blank lines and gaps.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Content clean-up

            
    
              
  • Shorten or remove technical/professional summaries when duplicated in position descriptions.
    • 
              
  • Remove company descriptions.
    • 
              
  • For positions more than 10 years old, shorten descriptions significantly or remove descriptions entirely.
    • 
              
  • Always retain company name, position title, and dates of employment.
    •

JobDiva documentation

          
Quick guides for required tracking.

          
Use these cards to log the key recruiting actions in JobDiva: submittals, interviews, prep calls, debrief close calls, references, and retention connects.

        

        

          

            
Submit to a job

            
    
              
  • Go to the candidate page.
    • 
              
  • Under Activities, click the paper icon with the up arrow.
    • 
              
  • Select the designated job.
    • 
              
  • Submit to the hiring manager to log an external submittal.
    • 
              
  • For MSP accounts, choose the blank option when needed.
    • 
              
  • Log quoted bill rate and agreed pay rate.
    • 
            

            
Download guide

          

          

            
Log an interview

            
    
              
  • Go to the candidate page.
    • 
              
  • Under Activities, click Set under the interview section for the designated job.
    • 
              
  • Select the interview date and time.
    • 
              
  • Confirm quoted bill rate and agreed pay rate are still correct.
    • 
            

            
Download guide

          

          

            
Interview prep in Diva

            
    
              
  • Open the candidate notes section and click the plus sign.
    • 
              
  • Set Action to Interview Prep.
    • 
              
  • Set Template to Interview Prep.
    • 
              
  • Link the note to an open job and tag recruiter or sales with @ when needed.
    •

    • 
              
  • Set Action to Debrief Close.
    •
