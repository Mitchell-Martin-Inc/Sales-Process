PIP Fit

Use the right lane

PIP may fit

  • Performance-based issue
  • Expectations can be defined
  • Improvement is realistic
  • Coaching has not been sufficient
  • Manager can support the process
  • Employee has shown ability and desire

PIP may not fit

  • Serious misconduct
  • Major policy violation
  • Trust is irreparably damaged
  • Repeated coaching already occurred
  • Role or business needs changed
  • Continued employment creates risk
